### Leading hypothesis for the pace collapse: minimum stay

- The comp set's **median booked length of stay is 3 nights**, every month for 24 months
  (`data/comps/market-history-24mo.csv`).
- The listing currently enforces **4 nights on most of Oct–Dec and 5 nights on Oct 9–18 and
  Oct 30–31**. A 4–5 night minimum excludes the market's median booking outright.
- October is where the pace gap is worst (19% vs 58% STLY) and it carries the highest
  minimums, while Aug/Sep — still at min-stay 2 — are the only months at or above last year.
- PriceLabs reports October bookings in this market typically land **6–25 days before stay**,
  so October is not yet lost; it is still inside the booking window.

**Evidence limit — do not over-read this.** The 2026-07-09 pull (`market-daily-60d.csv`)
shows min-stay 2 across every day it covered, but that pull only spanned Jul 9 – Sep 6, so it
says nothing about what October's minimum was in July. The correlation between high minimums
and weak pace is real; a recent *change* to those minimums is unproven. Confirm in the
PriceLabs min-stay settings before treating it as cause.

> restart the 90-day clock from the verified date.**

> analysis, tuning, or capex discussion, the configuration has to actually match what we
> believe we changed:

>    ceiling gap, concentrated in Nov 23–28 and Dec 20–31.

>    the 4–5 night minimums are intentional. If they are not deliberate, drop them toward the
>    market's 3-night median. Do not change anything else in the same sitting — one variable
>    at a time, or the next scorecard is unreadable again.
> 3. **Lower min price $332 → $315** as PriceLabs recommends. This is genuinely marginal
>    (the floor binds on 16 nights for $830 aggregate) and is listed only because it is free
>    and the pulse keeps flagging it. It is not the lever.
>
> - **Cost / new recurring burn:** $0.
> - **Projected impact:** cap fix is worth up to $9,395 on currently-open nights, realistically
>   a fraction of that since only booked nights pay. The min-stay question is plausibly worth
>   far more (October alone is ~12 nights behind last year), but it is unproven and must be
>   confirmed before it is claimed.
> - **Downside case:** raising the cap on a listing already pricing above market (PriceLabs:
>   "your prices are above the market" in both high and low season) risks suppressing
>   occupancy further. This is why the min-stay question matters more — and why the cap raise
>   should be judged on RevPAR, not ADR.
> - **Payback:** immediate; no outlay.
